The opportunity

Why Germany

Germany’s physician workforce is ageing, and hospitals are actively recruiting internationally trained doctors to keep specialist rosters full. That creates a genuine, structured pathway for MBBS graduates willing to learn the language and put in the years.

World-class infrastructure

Train in modern hospitals with advanced equipment and clinical technology that matches the best in the world.

Paid training from day one

PG training is structured as employment. You are not a student — you are a working doctor earning a salary from your first day of Junior Residency.

No entrance examination

There is no centralised entrance exam for PG admission in Germany. Placement runs on interview performance and your profile, hospital by hospital.

Facharzt title

On completing PG you’re awarded the Facharzt (specialist/consultant) title — a globally recognised medical credential.

Pathway to German Residency

Completing PG significantly improves your chances of Permanent German Residency.

The complete journey

The pathway, step by step

From the day you enrol to the day you earn your Facharzt title — every milestone in sequence, and what GooCampus handles at each one.

01
India7–9 months

German Language Training — A1 to B1

  • Online German classes from Level A1 to B1, conducted in India
  • Goethe-Institut A2 exam is mandatory — the certificate is required by the German Embassy for your visa

We provide: language training + A2 exam booking

02
India · parallel to Step 1~5 months

Degree Evaluation — Defizitbescheid

  • All academic documents — attested and notarised — are translated into German and submitted to the relevant German state authority
  • Runs parallel to language training; all originals must be submitted in full and on time to avoid delays

We provide: document preparation, translation and submission support

03
India~1 month

Receive Defizitbescheid & Apply for the German Visa

  • On receiving the Defizitbescheid, your complete visa file is compiled and submitted to the German Embassy
  • A blocked account must be opened at this stage as required by the Embassy

We provide: visa documentation, appointment scheduling, interview preparation

04
Germany4 months

Advanced Language Training — B2 & C1 (Doctors’ Course)

  • Specialised medical German course (B2, B2.2 medical terminology, C1) conducted in Germany — your primary preparation phase for the FSP
  • City registration and German bank account are set up during this period, with on-ground settlement support from arrival

We provide: institute admission, airport pickup, city registration, bank account, FSP prep

05
Germany1-hour exam

FSP Exam — Fachsprachprüfung

A structured, three-part medical German language exam to obtain the temporary licence (Berufserlaubnis) — patient case history (20 min), written documentation (20 min), and a doctor-to-doctor case conversation (20 min). On passing, a 2-year temporary medical licence is issued and you begin work in a teaching hospital, paid on the VKA scale, with OT and night shifts paid additionally.

We provide: FSP exam booking + preparation with a native German teacher

06
Germany · Junior Residencyup to 2 years

Junior Residency — Working as Assistant Doctor

  • Work as an assistant doctor in a teaching hospital under the temporary licence
  • Build clinical competency in your chosen specialisation alongside German colleagues, while preparing for the Approbation knowledge exam

We provide: interview preparation + hospital connection support

07
Germanytypically within Junior Residency

Approbation Exam — Permanent Licence & PG Entry

  • The Approbation knowledge exam formally recognises your medical degree across all of Germany
  • On passing, you receive a permanent medical licence and become eligible to begin PG — no entrance exam, placement is by profile and interview
  • PG runs 5–6 years depending on specialty, concluding with the Facharzt (specialist/consultant) title

We provide: Approbation documentation + exam preparation guidance

Final milestone

Approbation → Permanent Licence → PG → Facharzt Title. Exact Approbation timelines are set by the state (Bundesland) handling your case — your counsellor will confirm the specific window for your file.

Key clarifications on the package

  • The D16 visa fee is included in the package.
  • Ticket fare and accommodation charges are not included in the instalment package. Candidates use the amount deposited in the blocked account to manage accommodation and living expenses — GooCampus’s working range is roughly €1,000–1,500 per month, with the account expected to hold roughly €13,000–14,000 in total. The German government sets and reviews this figure annually by visa category, so confirm the exact current amount with the German mission handling your application before you fund the account.
  • The employment visa process begins only after one year. Its cost is not covered by GooCampus — doctors are required to pay those charges themselves.
